Import grades from Schoology

  1. In Schoology, click Grades in the top bar.
  2. Click each course name to open it. Courses start closed, and closed ones have no scores to copy.
  3. Press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C (on a Mac, ⌘A then ⌘C).
  4. Paste below and press Import grades.

Imports the current grading period. Scores and category weights replace any earlier Schoology import; grades you added yourself are kept. Import again whenever you want fresh grades.

Automatic sync with your Schoology API key Recommended

Brings in your classes, assignments, due dates, grades and what you've turned in, every time StudyFlow opens. Read-only: StudyFlow can't turn in, change or delete anything.

Sign in with Google at the top of Settings first. Your key is locked to your account.

  1. Open Schoology's API page (your Schoology address followed by /api) while signed in to Schoology.
  2. Copy the Consumer Key and the Consumer Secret and paste them below. Only paste them here, nowhere else.

Treat these like your password. StudyFlow locks (encrypts) them right away and only uses them to read your schoolwork.

Or use your calendar feed link (assignments and due dates only):

  1. Open Schoology and click Calendar in the top bar.
  2. Click Export at the top of the calendar (some schools call it Subscribe or iCal).
  3. Copy the link it shows. It starts with webcal://.
  4. Paste it below. StudyFlow connects as soon as the link is right.

Treat this link like a password: anyone with it can see your calendar.

Advanced: use your own relay

Leave this blank. Only fill it in if you run your own relay, such as a Cloudflare Worker.

No Export button? Your school may have turned it off. Download the calendar as an .ics file instead and use Import .ics file.
